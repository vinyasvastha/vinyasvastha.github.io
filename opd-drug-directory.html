Every Busy OPD Deserves a Quick, Reliable Memory

The OPD Drug Directory is an Android app developed by Vinyasvastha Trust for doctors working in busy outpatient departments. It lets a doctor build a personal directory that links symptoms, diseases, generic drugs, and brand names, so the right option comes to mind in seconds, even with a long queue of patients waiting.

Search any word and the app shows everything directly linked to it, plus the next level of related keywords. Link "Heart Burn" to a brand once, and searching that brand later shows Heart Burn too. The doctor's own clinical experience becomes a searchable, always-available reference.

🔒

100% Offline & Private

All keywords, links, and photos stay on the doctor's own phone. No account, no server, no tracking. Backups go only where the doctor chooses, such as their own Google Drive.

⚡

Instant Search

Live suggestions appear as you type, ranked like a modern search engine: exact match first, then names that start with your text, then everything else.

🪶

Light & Free

An app of under 1 MB that runs on Android 8 and above. No advertisements, no subscriptions, and no third-party libraries.

Key Features

Designed around how doctors actually think during an outpatient session.

🔗 Keyword-to-Keyword Linking

  • Direct links: every keyword linked to the one searched, A–Z. Links work both ways.
  • Indirect links: the next level of related keywords, grouped "via" each direct link.
  • Link many at once: type several names separated by commas; new ones are created automatically.

🏷️ Speciality Tags

  • Group by speciality: tag keywords as Pedia, ENT, Cardiology, or any name you like.
  • Tags window: browse each tag's keywords and search inside a single tag.
  • Easy upkeep: rename or delete a tag everywhere in one step.

📷 Drug Photos & Backup

  • Photos: snap a strip or carton with the camera and attach it to a keyword; images are shrunk to save space.
  • Quick backup: keywords and links as a small file.
  • Full backup: keywords, links, and photos, saved to Google Drive or anywhere on the phone.

📚 Built-in Reference Database Offline

  • Separate, read-only tab: never mixes with the doctor's own keywords.
  • PharmacotherapyDB (public domain): 91 diseases, 551 drugs, and about 1,145 disease–drug links, curated by physicians.
  • Two clear classes: disease-modifying and symptomatic drugs for each disease, all available offline.

Sister App: OPD Memory

Two names, one purpose.

🧠 OPD Memory Sister App

OPD Memory is the sister app of OPD Drug Directory, also developed by Vinyasvastha Trust. Both apps do the same job: they help doctors build a personal, offline memory of symptoms, diseases, generic drugs, and brand names, linked keyword to keyword for instant recall in the OPD.

Doctors can choose whichever of the two suits them. The same features, privacy promise, and offline design described on this page apply to both.

Who Benefits From OPD Drug Directory?

Faster recall, fewer slips, and more time for the patient.

🩺 OPD & Primary Care Doctors

Recall indications, generics, and local brand names in seconds during crowded outpatient sessions.

🏥 Rural & Government Health Centres

Works fully offline, so it stays useful in PHCs, CHCs, and camps with weak or no internet.

🎓 Interns & Medical Students

Build a personal pharmacology notebook during training that grows with clinical experience.

OPD Drug Directory is a memory aid for registered medical practitioners. It does not replace clinical judgement, current prescribing information, or national treatment guidelines.

Free & Open Public Welfare Initiative

OPD Drug Directory is developed and maintained by Vinyasvastha Trust purely as a public service. It contains no advertisements, no tracking, and no commercial subscription fees.
