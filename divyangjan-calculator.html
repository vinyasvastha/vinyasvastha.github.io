Empowering Divyangjan Through Accurate, Transparent Technology

The Divyangjan Calculator is an assistive software tool developed by Vinyasvastha Trust to simplify and standardize disability percentage calculations across India. Built strictly in compliance with the Government of India Gazette Notification S.O. 1338(E) (dated January 4, 2018) under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016, this utility provides an instant, transparent, and auditable computation of benchmark disabilities.

For decades, individuals with disabilities, social advocates, and certifying medical boards have had to navigate intricate mathematical formulas, complex combining equations, and extensive multi-schedule guidelines. Divyangjan Calculator solves this by automating the assessment logic while maintaining complete mathematical fidelity to the law.

Disability Categories Assessed

Comprehensive modules matching all guidelines specified under the RPwD Act schedules.

🦿 Locomotor & Orthopedic Impairments

  • Upper Limb Evaluation: Arm, forearm, hand component, and coordinated activities assessment.
  • Lower Limb Evaluation: Mobility components, muscle power loss, shortening, and joint stability.
  • Spine / Trunk Disabilities: Cervical, dorsal, and lumbar spine range and functional loss.
  • Amputations & Deformities: Codified benchmark percentage tables as per gazette schedules.

👁️ Visual & Sensory Impairments

  • Visual Impairment: Low vision and blindness assessment matching Snellen scale and visual field benchmarks.
  • Hearing Impairment: Pure tone audiometry decibel (dB) loss evaluation for both ears.
  • Speech & Language Disability: Voice production, fluency, and speech clarity scales.

🧠 Neurological & Chronic Conditions

  • Neurological Conditions: Multiple Sclerosis, Parkinson's disease, ataxia, and cerebral palsy assessment scales.
  • Intellectual & Learning Disabilities: Standardized diagnostic scales and adaptive functioning metrics.
  • Chronic Blood Conditions: Thalassemia, Hemophilia, and Sickle Cell disease benchmark provisions.

➕ Multiple Disabilities Formula Statutory

  • Combining Equation Implementation: Automates the statutory combining formula:
    a + [b × (90 - a) / 90]
  • Multi-System Calculation: Accurately cascades multiple impairments without mathematical discrepancy.
  • Benchmark Threshold Indicator: Instantly indicates whether the combined evaluation crosses the 40% benchmark threshold for statutory entitlements.
