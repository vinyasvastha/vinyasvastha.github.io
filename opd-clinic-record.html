OPD Clinic Record

A Simple, Offline Daily OPD Register for Doctors
A Healthcare Technology Initiative by Vinyasvastha Trust

Inquire / Early Access About the Trust

From Paper Register to Organised Digital Records

OPD Clinic Record is an Android app developed by Vinyasvastha Trust that replaces the handwritten OPD register. Each patient visit is entered in seconds, and the app builds a neat, dated Excel register for every day and every month. It is designed in line with the record-keeping guidelines of the National Medical Commission (NMC).

Doctors in busy clinics and health centres often struggle to keep complete, searchable records. OPD Clinic Record keeps the register simple to fill, easy to share, and safe on the doctor's own phone and Google Drive.

🔒

Offline-First & Private

Works fully without internet. Patient records stay on the doctor's phone and go only to the doctor's own Google Drive when the doctor chooses to upload.

📊

Excel-Ready Registers

A dated Excel sheet for every OPD day, and a monthly workbook with one tab per day plus a summary, ready for audits and reports.

☁️

Safe Drive Backup

Upload-only backup to Google Drive. The app never deletes anything from Drive, so older records stay safe.

Key Features

Everything a daily OPD register needs, in four simple tabs.

✏️ Quick Patient Entry

  • All register fields: serial number and date filled automatically, plus name, age/sex, mobile, address, provisional diagnosis, and clinical notes / Rx.
  • Diagnosis shortcuts: save common diagnoses once and add them with a tap.
  • Three ways to capture: camera, gallery, or voice dictation.

📖 Daily Register & Excel

  • Today's Register: see the day's patients in a clear table, or load any earlier date.
  • Daily sheet: export the day as an Excel file named by date.
  • Monthly workbook: combine every day of a month into one file with a monthly summary.

🗂️ Two Storage Modes

  • Compact mode: keeps only the Excel register and clears temporary photos and voice notes, so the phone never fills up.
  • Full archive mode: also keeps prescription photos and audio, each named by date, patient, and diagnosis for easy search.
  • Drive upload: daily sheets, monthly workbooks, media, or a complete backup, one tap each.

🤖 Optional AI Assistant Optional

  • Manual by default: every feature works without AI and without any key.
  • Bring your own key: a doctor can add their own free AI key (such as Google Gemini) to fill the form from voice or a prescription photo.
  • Doctor stays in control: AI is used only when the doctor turns it on, and every entry can be checked before saving.

Who Benefits From OPD Clinic Record?

Better records today mean better care tomorrow.

🩺 Private Clinic Doctors

Keep a complete, dated register of every OPD visit without paperwork piling up.

🏥 Health Centres & Camps

Works offline in PHCs, CHCs, and medical camps, and produces Excel sheets ready for monthly reporting.

📁 Patients & Continuity of Care

Well-kept records help doctors find past visits quickly and give better follow-up care.

The doctor remains responsible for the accuracy and safe keeping of patient records. Any AI-filled entry should be checked before saving.
