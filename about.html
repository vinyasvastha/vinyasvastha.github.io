About Vinyasvastha Trust

A registered public charitable trust in Delhi, India, dedicated to holistic social welfare, legal empowerment, digital inclusion, and sustainable community development.

Who We Are

VINYASVASTHA TRUST is a non-governmental, non-profit public charitable institution registered in New Delhi, India. Founded upon the foundational principle of universal human dignity, our trust works at grassroots, academic, and policy levels to alleviate systemic hardship, empower disenfranchised communities, and build sustainable pathways for societal advancement.

We believe that genuine, long-term societal transformation demands an integrated approach. Rather than addressing disadvantages in isolation, Vinyasvastha Trust unites legal literacy, education, public health, technological empowerment, livelihood coaching, ecological sustainability, and social justice into a unified development model.

🏛️ Institutional Profile & Governance

  • Legal Status: Registered Public Charitable Trust under the Indian Trusts Act, 1882.
  • Jurisdiction: Registered & Operating in New Delhi, National Capital Territory of Delhi, India.
  • Operational Reach: Pan-India public welfare programs, community outreach camps, and digital accessibility tools.
  • Governing Philosophy: 100% non-commercial, non-sectarian public service dedicated to the welfare of all citizens.

Vision & Mission

Our moral compass guiding every grassroots initiative and policy intervention.

🎯 Our Vision

An equitable, dignified, and harmonious society where every citizen—regardless of caste, religion, gender, socio-economic status, or physical ability—has unhindered access to justice, education, healthcare, technology, and economic opportunities to lead a fulfilling life.

🚀 Our Mission

To implement transparent, community-driven social welfare programmes, legal aid and rights literacy drives, scientific and technological solutions for accessibility, public healthcare initiatives, and ecological conservation projects across India.

Aims & Objectives of the Trust

As codified in our founding deed, Vinyasvastha Trust pursues 8 core objectives for the holistic development of society:

1. ⚖️ Legal Awareness & Justice

Promoting constitutional and legal awareness, advancing access to justice for underserved populations, conducting citizen rights education, and facilitating structured rehabilitation and reintegration programs for prisoners.

2. 🔬 Science & Technology Featured

Building impactful software and assistive tech tools that ease the lives of marginalized citizens. Our flagship digital tool "Divyangjan Calculator" provides an offline, accurate assessment benchmark under the RPwD Act 2016.

3. 📚 Education & Digital Literacy

Eradicating educational disparities through community literacy drives, digital skills workshops, vocational training for underprivileged youth, and learning resource distribution.

4. 👩‍👧 Child & Women Welfare

Safeguarding child rights, preventing exploitation, fostering female literacy and economic independence, promoting maternal health, and protecting women against violence and discrimination.

5. 🩺 Healthcare & Nutrition

Organizing free preventive health check-up camps, combating chronic malnutrition among women and children, supporting healthcare access for remote populations, and raising community health awareness.

6. 🤝 Inclusivity & Disability Empowerment

Dedicated empowerment initiatives for Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan), tribal communities, minorities, and backward classes to ensure equal participation in national growth.

7. 🌾 Skill Development & Rural Livelihoods

Supporting sustainable agriculture, cottage industries, vocational skills, rural entrepreneurship, self-help groups, and micro-enterprises to generate sustainable rural livelihoods.

8. 🌱 Ecology & Clean Energy

Championing environmental protection through massive afforestation, water conservation, community sanitation, waste management, and adoption of clean and renewable solar energy.

Charter of Non-Discrimination

In accordance with our registered deed, all charitable programmes, public welfare activities, legal aid initiatives, educational drives, and community empowerment operations conducted by VINYASVASTHA TRUST are executed strictly for the benefit of the general public without discrimination based on caste, creed, religion, gender, or social background.
