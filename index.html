About Vinyasvastha Trust

Registered in Delhi, India, Vinyasvastha Trust is a non-profit charitable institution established to foster holistic social, educational, economic, and environmental transformation. We work tirelessly to eliminate systemic barriers and build a brighter, equitable future for every citizen without discrimination based on caste, creed, religion, gender, or social background.

Our Core Focus Areas

Our comprehensive public welfare mandate addresses key developmental pillars across urban and rural India.

⚖️ Legal Awareness & Justice

Promoting access to legal justice, citizen rights education, and structured rehabilitation and reintegration programs for prisoners.

🔬 Science & Technology

We build accessible apps that make life easier for marginalized citizens and the common public. Our premier application "Divyangjan Calculator" is going live soon.

📚 Education & Digital Literacy

Eradicating learning disparities through digital training, foundational skill acquisition, vocational coaching, and youth empowerment.

🩺 Healthcare & Nutrition

Organizing essential medical drives, malnutrition relief, maternal-child health support, and public wellness awareness campaigns.

🤝 Social Inclusivity & PwDs

Empowerment initiatives tailored for persons with disabilities (Divyangjan), tribal groups, minorities, and marginalized demographics.

🌱 Rural & Green Ecology

Championing sustainable agriculture, rural employment, renewable energy adoption, community sanitation, and environmental conservation.
